# Lites trademark policy

Code licences do not grant trademark rights. “Lites”, the Lites word mark, logo, distinctive product marks and official service branding may be owned or claimed by the rights holder identified in `config/legal-governance.json` once that record is active.

You may truthfully state that unmodified software is “Lites”, link to this repository, describe compatibility as “for Lites”, and retain required attribution and legal notices. Community forks may say they are “based on Lites” if that wording is no more prominent than the fork's own name.

You may not imply sponsorship, certification, official hosting or endorsement; use Lites branding as your product, company, domain or social identity; modify the logo and present it as official; or use confusingly similar branding for a hosted service or commercial distribution without written permission. Modified distributions must use a distinct primary name and clearly identify their changes and source.

Security research, commentary, comparison and nominative references remain allowed where applicable law permits. Trademark permission is separate from AGPL compliance and commercial licensing. Questions or permission requests use the contact configured in the active legal-governance record; while that identity is unconfigured, no new trademark permission can be issued.
