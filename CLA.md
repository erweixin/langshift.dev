# Lites Contributor License Agreement policy

Lites uses a contributor licence agreement so the copyright owner can continue distributing the same maintained work under AGPL-3.0 and separately negotiated commercial terms. Contributors retain ownership of their original work.

The agreement follows the Harmony Individual and Entity Contributor License Agreement model: a contributor grants the configured Lites rights holder a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, transferable, royalty-free and irrevocable copyright licence, including sublicensing rights, plus the patent licence needed to make, use and distribute the contribution. The contributor represents that they are entitled to grant those rights, identifies third-party material, and provides the contribution without a support or warranty obligation. Entity submissions must be signed by an authorized representative and must identify covered contributors.

The legally operative rights holder, address, governing law and submission address are taken from `config/legal-governance.json` and reproduced in the signed agreement. While that configuration has status `identity_required`, no external contribution is accepted and no click-through or pull-request checkbox is treated as a signed CLA. Private keys, signatures, residential addresses and signed forms are never committed to this public repository.

## Acceptance procedure

1. Obtain the current Individual or Entity agreement generated from the active legal-governance configuration.
2. Sign it by handwritten signature, a recognized electronic-signature service, or a verifiable detached digital signature.
3. Submit it only through the configured private submission address.
4. Wait for confirmation that the exact GitHub identity and agreement version are active in the private CLA registry.
5. In each pull request, attest that the contribution is covered by that active agreement and disclose any employer or third-party rights.

A CLA does not replace the outbound AGPL-3.0 licence, the contribution review process, or required notices. The policy is based on the [Harmony Agreements](https://www.harmonyagreements.org/agreements) contributor-licence model; the project-specific signed agreement controls if its terms differ from this process summary.
